I don't produce the dashboard, and here that limit is doing you a favour,
because "our users said they want a dashboard" is almost never a request for a
dashboard.

It is usually one of three things wearing the same words. A reporting request:
somebody has to produce a number for somebody else on a schedule and is doing it
by hand. A monitoring request: somebody is afraid of one specific thing going
wrong and has no way to find out early. Or a trust request: people do not
believe the system is doing what it says and want to watch it. Those produce
different products. The first is an export. The second is an alert. Only the
third is a screen people look at, and it is the rarest of the three.

What I would ask before anything is drawn:

- What did the person do the last time they needed this, and what did it cost
  them?
- Who reads the output, and what do they do differently depending on what it
  says?
- If the answer is "nothing differently", you have a reporting obligation rather
  than a decision, and the cheapest honest solution is a file on a schedule.

I can't tell you what belongs on it or how it should be arranged, because that
follows from the decision it serves and nobody has named the decision yet. Once
you have, the arrangement follows from it rather than from taste, and I can
reason with you about the trade-offs in whatever arrangement you propose.
