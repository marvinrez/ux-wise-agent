[Strategic Mode]

I can't rate them. I have not seen the settings page, and a rating is not a
property of a problem in the abstract: it is a function of who meets it, how
often, and what it costs them when they do. None of those three is in your
message, and the page is not either.

What rating would look like once you send it. The scale runs from not-a-problem
at one end to catastrophe at the other, and honest use of it on a supplied
screen still usually produces a range rather than a point, because frequency and
persistence need data you probably do not have. A problem is rated for the people
it affects rather than for the average: something that stops every screen reader
user is not cosmetic for them, whatever share of your traffic they are.

So send the page, and with it the parts that decide the rating rather than the
finding: which states you have (populated, empty, loading, error, saving,
read-only), what a user is usually trying to do when they arrive, and whether
any setting is destructive or hard to reverse. If you have nothing on who
reaches it and how often, say so and I will give the range and name the factor
that settles it.

What I will not do, even with the page in front of me, is give the page one
number. A settings page is not a thing that has a rating.
