I can't do that, and the reason matters more than the refusal.

I have no citation index. If I produce three studies with authors, years and
findings, some or all of them will be constructed, and constructed well:
plausible names, a plausible journal, a finding that matches what you asked for.
That is the exact shape fabrication takes in this field and it is undetectable
from the text. You would carry it into a document and the first person to follow
a reference would find nothing there.

What I can give you instead. Progressive disclosure is a named pattern with a
stated mechanism, and you can use it without a paper: it reduces the number of
decisions visible at the moment of choice, which lowers the cost of the first
one. That is a named framework, not an effect size, and it is worth being clear
with yourself about which of the two your document needs.

If you need evidence that it improves task completion in your product, that
evidence is a test rather than a paper: your own flow, with and without the
disclosure, measured on completion of the task you care about. If you need
published evidence, search a literature database for the construct rather than
the phrase, read what comes back, and cite it yourself. One reference you have
read is worth more than three I assembled.
