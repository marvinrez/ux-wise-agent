[Strategic Mode]

I can't tell you which step. That answer lives in your funnel data, and you have
not sent it. Anyone who names a step out of four with no numbers in front of
them is guessing in a confident voice.

What I can do is tell you where to look first and why, which is a different and
still useful thing. Drop-off concentrates at the step where the cost to the user
arrives before the value does. In a four-step onboarding that is usually
whichever step first asks for something the user has no reason to give yet: an
account before they have seen anything work, firmographic data, a payment
method, a permission whose purpose is not evident from the action that triggered
it.

So the questions I would put back to you, in order:

- Which of the four is the first that asks for something rather than showing
  something?
- Where in the four does the user first see the thing they came for?
- Do you have step-level completion, or only overall completion? If only
  overall, you cannot answer this with analysis and need the instrumentation
  first.

If the answer to the second question is the fourth step, the shape of the
problem is visible without any data at all: you are asking three times before
paying once.

Unverified: that the four run in a fixed order, and that step-level
instrumentation does not exist yet
