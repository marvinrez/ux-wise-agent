# Starter Prompts — UX Wise AI Agent v2.0

**Curated by Marcos Rezende**
[marcosrezende.com](https://marcosrezende.com)

---

These prompts are designed to activate the full reasoning capacity of UX Wise AI — Agent. Each one is structured to produce a substantive response rather than a summary. Pair them with a mode declaration for best results.

Mode markers: `[S]` = Strategic, `[D]` = Direct, `[P]` = Provocative, `[R]` = Research.
If no mode is specified, the agent infers from context and defaults to Strategic.

## Understanding UX Problems

- What are the hidden friction points in **[insert user flow]** that might cause drop-offs? `[S]`
- What early indicators suggest that a UX strategy is failing? `[S]`
- How do I identify if a user journey is too complex? `[D]`
- What cognitive biases should I consider when designing **[insert experience]**? `[S]`
- What psychological principles apply to **[insert user experience]**? `[S]`
- What factors negatively affect user retention in **[insert industry]** that teams typically overlook? `[S]`
- How do I design for cognitive load reduction in **[insert feature or product]**? `[S]`
- What are the common usability failure modes in **[insert industry or product type]**? `[S]`
- What factors should I consider when designing for **[insert user group or accessibility need]**? `[S]`
- How do I improve the discoverability of **[insert feature or product]**? `[D]`
- What are the signs that a UX flow needs a redesign versus needs better onboarding? `[S]`
- What are the biggest UX constraints when designing for **[insert platform]**? `[S]`

## Research and Validation

## How to Use These Prompts

Replace `[bracketed text]` with your specific product, feature, platform, or context. The more specific your substitution, the more precise the response. Vague inputs produce vague outputs.

**Weak input:**
"What are the best UX patterns for navigation?"

**Strong input:**
"What are the best UX patterns for navigation in a B2B analytics dashboard where users hold three distinct roles with different data access levels, and the current left sidebar is being deprecated due to screen real estate constraints on 13-inch laptops?"

The second version gives the agent enough constraint to take a real position rather than listing general options.

**For Research Mode:** Specify what decision the research needs to inform, not just the research question. "Design a usability test for our onboarding" is weaker than "Design a usability test to determine whether our onboarding drop-off at step 3 is caused by confusion about what the product does, or by a technical barrier during account setup."
